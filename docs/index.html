01 / Project overview

One project. Multiple paths
to legal discovery.

LegalBuddy brings several components together in an ongoing build. These are project directions reflected in the repository—not a claim that every feature is production-ready.

01 — ASSIST
✳

Conversational assistant

A chat-oriented interface designed to let users ask legal questions and explore information in a more natural way.

02 — EXPLORE
⌕

Case & law insights

Backend modules and data-ingestion scripts support exploration of cases, legal documents, and relevant judgments.

03 — CONNECT
◇

Lawyer recommendations

Recommendation-related model artifacts and feature-importance data support experimentation with lawyer discovery.

02 / Conceptual flow

From a question to
a clearer next step.

A high-level view of the intended experience. The precise flow may differ between repository components and requires validation against a running deployment.

STEP 01

Ask or explore

Start with a legal question or a discovery need.

STEP 02

Retrieve context

Explore relevant content and supporting case material where available.

STEP 03

Surface insights

Organize useful information and recommendations for review.

STEP 04

Review carefully

Verify sources and consult a qualified legal professional for advice.
